Greek Salad Recipe

Description

Greek salad is the epitomy of the Mediterranean saladry.

Simple recipe again, using only fresh ingredients and local sourced products. Simplicity at its best

Ingredients

  • Olives
  • Olive oil
  • Feta cheese
  • Tomatoes
  • Cucumber
  • Onions
  • Oregano
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Cut your onions, cucumbers and tomatos in dices
  2. Cut your feta cheese in dices as well
  3. Throw them in a large bowl and also throw some olives in it
  4. Drown the bowl in olive oil
  5. Season with salt and oregano
