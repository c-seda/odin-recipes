Tzatziki Recipe

Description

You can eat it as a sauce or a standalone plate, doesn't matter. It screams of Greece.

Based on yogurt, the origin of which is disputed. So what did the Greeks do? Made a version of yogurt entirely theirs, they made tzatziki.

Ingredients

  • 2% fat yogurt
  • Cucumber
  • Grape vinegar
  • Salt
  • Garlic
  • Black pepper
  • Dill

Steps

  1. Grate the cucumber
  2. Place the grated cucumber in a cloth and squeeze the excessive cucumber liquid out.
  3. Throw the grated cucumber in a bowl with the yogurt and the garlic.
  4. Mix them altogether and finish with black pepper, olive oil, vinegar and salt.
