Pitogyro Recipe

Description

Pitogyro, or pita gyros, is the most famous Greek street food. You can see Greeks of all economic classes drooling all over it and if I may say, it is the most anti-discriminatory food, as everyone sets their differences aside when it comes to enjoying a pitogyro together!

Its origins are unknown but I've heard from a taxi driver that Greeks invented it during the conquest of Troy.

The recipe is simple, yet the flavor is wild. Charcoal grilled pita bread with fresh vegetables, the sauce of your preference and last but not least, either chicken gyro meat or pork gyro meat. Let's get into the details, shouldn't we?

Ingredients

  • Pita bread
  • Chicken/pork gyro meat (or both if you are meraklis)
  • Onions
  • Tomato slices
  • Potato fries
  • The sauce of your preference for it to slip (Tzatziki, Tyrokafteri, Ouggareza, Ketchup, Mustard)
  • Black pepper
  • Salt
  • Oregano

Steps

  1. Fire up the charcoal grill.
  2. Toss the pita bread onto the charcoal grill and flip until golden and fragrant.
  3. Spread the sauce of your preference throughout the surface of the pita bread.
  4. Toss in the fries, the tomato slices and the onions.
  5. Cut your spinning meat in thin slices and then place it onto the pita bread.
  6. Wrap the pita bread (180 iQ required)
  7. Serve with an ice cold Coca Cola for maximum pleasure.
